How to read recent form

Recent strength ranks all 32 defenses using the same equal-weight run, net pass and total points allowed method, limited to each team's last three completed games. Rank movement compares recent rank with season rank; it is relative to other defenses. Opponent quality is not adjusted. The streaming fit score continues to use season strength.
